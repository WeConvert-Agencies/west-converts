# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners of trades businesses (HVAC first; also roofing, plumbing and electrical). Hands-on operators,
often on a phone between jobs, frequently burned by agencies before. They evaluate the site when a
West Converts cold call or ad makes them ask "are these guys real?", so the page has to earn trust fast.

## Product Purpose
West Converts gets trades businesses more booked jobs. Meta and Google ads are the flagship,
highest-revenue service; web design and the AI Receptionist are real, active services sold alongside
ads as a hybrid offer. Success on the site is a booked free strategy call.

## Positioning
Straight answers instead of a sales pitch, and a direct line to the people doing the work, with the
founder personally overseeing every project. Leads go straight to the client and are never pooled or
resold; the client owns their site, domain and Google Business Profile.

## Operating Context
Currently based in Fort Collins, Colorado and expanding out of state. Regional framing (Fort Collins,
Loveland, Greeley, the service-area list) belongs only in the founder/About section and the footer,
never in the hero or top-level trust bullets. Primary conversion: "Book Your Free Strategy Call" and
the contact form at the bottom of the page.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Core services: Meta & Google Ads (flagship), Web Design & Build, AI Receptionist.
- Listed sub-services: Ad Creative (graphics & video), Tracking & Reporting (monthly reporting,
  AI-assisted analysis), Landing Pages, AI Website Chatbot (premium web add-on; site shows a
  front-end-only mockup, not wired to a live backend), Google Business Profile & Local SEO,
  Brand & Growth Strategy, Logo Design.
- No prices on the public site. Every service is custom quote via "Book Your Free Strategy Call".
- Contact form and AI chat widget are not connected to a backend yet.
- Deployed as a single static HTML file (Netlify drag-and-drop); every image must be embedded.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: West Converts (formerly WeConvert).
- Team voice ("we"). Danny Tellez is the founder who personally oversees every project, described as
  a young entrepreneur based in Fort Collins. Never mention that he is a student.
- Never use "AI-powered" or "AI-run" as a headline or above-the-fold claim; AI appears only as a
  functional label (AI Receptionist, AI Website Chatbot, AI-assisted reporting).
- Primary CTA wording sitewide: "Book Your Free Strategy Call".

## Evidence on Hand
- Hot & Cold Mechanical (Longmont, CO): real, live client website, hotncoldmechanical.com; screenshot
  embedded in the site. Only client logo cleared for the logo strip.
- No testimonials, results, client counts, stats or years in business exist yet. Do not fabricate them;
  testimonial slots stay clearly marked placeholders.
- No original technician photography in the repo yet; the hero photo is a labeled placeholder until
  real Hot & Cold photos are supplied.

## Product Principles
1. Earn trust before asking for anything: proof and plain answers over claims.
2. Lead with the outcome (booked jobs), not the mechanism (AI, ads jargon).
3. Nothing the site says may be unverifiable; placeholders are labeled as placeholders.
4. The founder's oversight and a direct line are the differentiator versus typical agencies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA is required: contrast on every text/background and button/background pairing, full
keyboard navigation, and mobile touch targets of at least 44×44px.
