## Business Model (updated 2026-08-30 — hybrid, not phased)
WeConvert now runs a hybrid model, not a strict phase-gated one:

1. **WeConvert – Web Design** (active): Build websites for local trades businesses (painting, HVAC, landscaping, etc.) around Northern Colorado with weak or no website. Approach: find the business → build a demo site → cold call the owner directly (not employees) → offer to show them the demo.
2. **WeConvert – Marketing Services (Ads)** (active, in testbed phase): Running Meta/Google ads is now sold together with Web Design as a hybrid offer, not gated behind "5 clients first" anymore. Currently proving the ads product on two free/practice HVAC clients — Hot & Cold Mechanical and NuWave — for roughly 1-2 months before confidently selling ads to paying clients. Cold-call scripts stay web-design-only during this window; ads only come up if a prospect raises it first, framed as "coming soon," never with a committed date.
3. **WeConvert – Lead Response Systems**: shelved for now, not an active priority. Revisit only if there's spare bandwidth later.

Lead sourcing is no longer geographically range-limited to "near Longmont only" — sourcing now covers all of Colorado. Note: in-person demo walk-ins still depend on driving distance; statewide sourcing is mainly for cold-call outreach.
